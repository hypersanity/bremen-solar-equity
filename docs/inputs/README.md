# The inputs

These are the only human inputs to the study. The brief is reproduced verbatim, typos included, because this repository records what the agent was given.

## 1. Research brief (first message)

> Conduct a succinct study, let’s say around 5000-7000 words, with a full scale GIS analysis on solar equity in the city state Bremen.
>
> Your job is threefold:
>
> 1. Conduct research on factors that go into determining a households access to solar power by factoring in things like upfront costs, installation fees, bureaucratic hurdles and opportunities (Regulatory), grid infrastructure by neighborhood, price appeal short vs long term compared to paying energy bills from the grid.
>
> 2. Conduct a practical study on solar equity in Bremen, where you determine which neighborhoods have higher shares of solar panels relative to number households, and make sure that you exclusively focus on residential energy access. Write a methodology section and analytical section, make sure to take screenshots, and add at least 1 chloropleth map with a readable legend, and readable tables where necessary.
>
> 3. Attempt to explain the gaps in equity, but try to focus on grounded citations rather than pure speculation. If there is some speculation then be explicit and explain the reasoning.
>
> Note: Try to find the data files yourself, if you continue to struggle accessing specific files, let me now and I’ll try to download them manually for you. Before you start the project, do some research first to plan out your methodology (e.g. which data you think are suitable to conduct this research, whether you want to use pythonGIS and build an interactive web version or just a plane map you can paste into report). You may also improve the research plan, if you think anything is missing.
